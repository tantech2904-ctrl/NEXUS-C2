MASTER BUILD BRIEF
==============================================================

PROJECT
==============================================================

Build a production-quality, fully interactive browser-based training
simulation for Smart India Hackathon 2026 problem statement SIH26248:

"Immersive Multi-Domain Decision-Making Trainer for Degraded Communication
Environments"

PRODUCT NAME
==============================================================

COMMSHIELD
Degraded Command Decision Trainer

Use the product name throughout the UI.

Tagline:

"DECIDE UNDER UNCERTAINTY."

Secondary descriptor:

"Immersive multi-domain command training for degraded information
environments."

==============================================================
PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
==============================================================

Do NOT build a generic administrative dashboard.

Build an immersive command-and-control TRAINING SIMULATOR.

The user must feel as though they have entered a professional command
training environment.

The central experience is:

START SCENARIO
        ↓
NORMAL INFORMATION FLOW
        ↓
ENVIRONMENTAL / COMMUNICATION EVENT
        ↓
INFORMATION QUALITY DEGRADES
        ↓
REPORTS ARRIVE LATE
        ↓
REPORTS DISAPPEAR
        ↓
REPORTS CONFLICT
        ↓
THE TRAINEE MUST DECIDE
        ↓
SYSTEM SIMULATES CONSEQUENCE
        ↓
AFTER-ACTION REVIEW
        ↓
PERFORMANCE SCORE
        ↓
INSTRUCTOR ANALYTICS

The experience must be understandable within 15 seconds by a judge who
has never seen the application before.

==============================================================
IMPORTANT PRODUCT BOUNDARY
==============================================================

This is a training and decision-support simulation.

Do NOT use classified information.

Do NOT claim that synthetic units, locations, command structures, or
events represent real military deployments.

Do NOT reproduce actual military facility coordinates, force dispositions,
weapon employment procedures, targeting workflows, or operational plans.

Use fictional training formations, fictional command callsigns, and
fictional operational objectives.

Use public historical disaster/communications datasets ONLY to calibrate
environmental stress, communication degradation, recovery behaviour,
geospatial context, and information-reliability concepts.

Every historical scenario must contain a visible provenance label:

"HISTORICAL BASIS — PUBLIC DATA"

and:

"Command entities and scenario injects are synthetic training constructs."

==============================================================
SIMULATION MATHEMATICS
==============================================================

Make the simulation transparent and deterministic.

Example communication quality:

Q = A * (1 - packetLoss) * latencyFactor * bandwidthFactor

where:

A = channel availability

latencyFactor decreases as latency rises.

Then:

InformationConfidence =
SourceReliability
* CommunicationQuality
* Freshness
* Consistency

DecisionScore should combine:

decisionQuality
timeliness
sourceEvaluation
coordination
adaptability
riskAwareness

Provide comments/documentation explaining these equations.

Do NOT make the system claim that these formulas are doctrinal military
scoring standards.

They are the simulator's training heuristics.

==============================================================
MOST IMPORTANT DESIGN PRINCIPLE
==============================================================

Do not optimise for the number of features.

Optimise for this experience:

"Something just went wrong.
I no longer trust my information.
I have seconds to decide.
What do I do?"

That moment must feel exceptionally polished.

The judge should immediately understand the problem, experience the
problem, make a decision inside the problem, and see measurable
training feedback.

Build the product around that loop.

==============================================================
NON-NEGOTIABLE SIGNATURE FEATURES
==============================================================

The following two features are the defining innovations of COMMSHIELD
and MUST be implemented as fully functional features, not mockups.

1. INFORMATION CONFIDENCE ENGINE

Every meaningful information item must visibly expose:

- source
- timestamp
- age
- communication channel
- source reliability
- channel quality
- freshness
- consistency
- current confidence
- verification state

The UI must dynamically communicate confidence degradation.

Example:

FIELD REPORT
SOURCE: FIELD TEAM ALPHA
AGE: 18.4 SEC
CHANNEL: DEGRADED RADIO

CONFIDENCE
██████░░░░ 61%

If a contradiction occurs, confidence must change dynamically.

The trainee must be able to understand WHY information became
less trustworthy.

Do not merely display a generic "LOW CONFIDENCE" badge.

--------------------------------------------------------------

2. DECISION REPLAY / INFORMATION-STATE RECONSTRUCTION

After every major decision, the instructor must be able to select:

[REPLAY DECISION]

The replay timeline must reconstruct the EXACT INFORMATION STATE
that existed at the moment the trainee made the decision.

At that timestamp display:

- map state
- communication state
- available reports
- unavailable reports
- report age
- confidence values
- contradictions
- network status
- environmental conditions
- decision options available
- trainee-selected decision
- trainee rationale

The instructor must be able to scrub backward and forward through
the timeline.

Most importantly:

DO NOT reveal information to the trainee during replay that was not
available to them at the original decision timestamp.

The replay must answer:

"WHAT DID THE TRAINEE KNOW WHEN THEY MADE THE DECISION?"

This is a core training capability, not merely a visual animation.

==============================================================
FINAL ACCEPTANCE TEST
==============================================================

A build is NOT considered complete unless a judge can:

1. Start a scenario.
2. Observe communication degradation.
3. Observe information confidence change.
4. Receive conflicting information.
5. Make a decision.
6. Enter a rationale.
7. Receive a measurable score.
8. Open the AAR.
9. Click REPLAY DECISION.
10. Scrub to the exact decision moment.
11. See exactly what information was available at that moment.
12. Understand why the simulator evaluated the decision as it did.

These 12 actions constitute the minimum viable end-to-end demonstration
of SIH26248.
